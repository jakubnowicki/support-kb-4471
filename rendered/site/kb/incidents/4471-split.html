The three problems

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. A day-first tenant’s dates come out with day and month swapped, on every channel and both booking forms.
  • hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People at day-first tenants type what the hint says, and the form reads it day first. Only reports sent through the form, only at tenants on booking-v3. The parser fix leaves this as it is; the hint has to change.
  • one-day: the date moves by one day, not by a month swap. Reporters describe Monday for Tuesday, a client in Toronto getting a different day than one in Lisbon, an invite that arrives before the booking. That points at time zones, not at date order. The desk had footnoted these under parser.
